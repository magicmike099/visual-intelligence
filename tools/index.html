How to get my saved places from Google Maps
  1. On a computer, open takeout.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click Deselect all, then tick Maps (your places). This holds your starred and labelled places with their coordinates.
  3. Create the export and download it. This can take a few minutes.
  4. Open the zip and send the places file (usually Saved Places.json) to this phone, then tap Import a file.

The Saved option exports your lists as CSV files that usually have no coordinates, so those places cannot be placed on the map. You can also paste the full link of a place (the long google.com/maps link, not a short maps.app.goo.gl link), or search for it above. Your places stay on this phone and are never uploaded.
